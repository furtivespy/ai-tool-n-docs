## Prompt
Every weekday at 8:45 AM ET, send Will a short daily deal roundup in this chat. Read-only: never sign in, sign up, add to cart, or buy.

WHAT WILL WANTS (recall memory for his current interests):
1. First, rare, limited, or event-style deals: product drops like the Home Depot Holiday Sweater, one-day specials, record-low or "best price we've seen" deals, limited-stock items, and upcoming timed drops worth planning for.
2. Kids' games and toys that are genuinely worth it (LEGO only when unusually good, about 40%+ off).
3. Homeowner tools, deck, and house gear (he's planning black string-light poles for a wood deck, so no rush).
4. Smart home sensors and gadgets.
Skip anything that's on sale all the time, and skip Halloween candy for 2026. Don't build sections around past purchases. On a slow day, send just a few items and don't pad.

SOURCES: the Slickdeals frontpage RSS (slickdeals.net/newsearch.php?mode=frontpage&searcharea=deals&searchin=first&rss=1, plus keyword RSS with &q=), DealNews RSS (dealnews.com/?rss=1), Meh (meh.com/feed.json), Tanga (tanga.com; Will likes it), Woot, Best Buy Deal of the Day, Home Depot Special Buy of the Day, Newegg Shell Shocker, and 9to5Toys. Woot, Best Buy, Home Depot, and Newegg may need a real browser. Amazon and Walmart block automated access, so get their deals from Slickdeals and DealNews posts and don't try to get around their blocks.

VETTING: flag inflated "list" or "was" prices, skip third-party markups, note promo codes and expirations, and never invent prices. Include when you checked (ET).

FORMAT: friendly and skimmable, with about 5–10 highlights. Lead with the rare or limited deals. For each: item, price, why it's good, retailer, expiry or limits, and a markdown link. Mention the Meh, Woot, and Tanga deals of the day in one line only if they're interesting. End with a one-line heads-up on upcoming sale events when relevant.
